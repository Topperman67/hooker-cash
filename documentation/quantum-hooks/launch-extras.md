# Launch extras

## Superposition

Split the creator share across up to four extra wallets, for example a co-founder, an artist or a community treasury.

Each wallet's share is fixed at launch. In every pulse, the Superposition wallets are paid their share of the creator slice first, and the launcher gets the rest.

## Time Dilation

Lock part of your dev buy with a cliff and linear vesting. Locked tokens can only be released to you, on schedule.

* By default, the Launch page locks 50% of the dev buy with a 7-day cliff and 90 days of vesting.
* The cliff must end before vesting finishes.
* Vesting lasts at least one day and at most four years.

The lock is applied in the launch flow right after the coin is created.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Like the hook allocation, Superposition wallets and Time Dilation schedules can't be changed after launch.
{% endhint %}
