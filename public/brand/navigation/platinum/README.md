# Hookbrew platinum navigation

Nine original transparent PNG icons generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-17. These replace the earlier vibrant sidebar artwork. All nine destinations have a dedicated image; Trade is opposing exchange arrows and Agents is a robot head.

## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single premium 3D navigation icon for Hookbrew, a professional finance application. Square PNG, genuine transparent alpha background, no opaque backdrop or checkerboard artwork. Material: satin frosted platinum glass, mostly opaque smoke-silver body, soft brushed pale silver edges, tiny desaturated sage reflections, extremely restrained lavender shadow. Calm neutral professional palette, NOT bright blue, NOT neon mint, NOT rainbow, NO glow. Substantial rounded geometry, simple unmistakable silhouette legible at 32 pixels, tasteful shallow dimensionality, soft top-left studio light, near-front view. Centered icon occupying exactly about 76 percent of square canvas with clear transparent margin on all sides. No pedestal unless explicitly part of subject, no floor, no frame, no text, no watermark, no brand logos. Isolated object. Consistent family of professional sidebar icons.

## Per-image subject prompts

- **overview.png:** Four separated rounded rectangular dashboard tiles arranged in a balanced 2 by 2 grid. Two taller and two shorter tiles within one compact square silhouette; clean executive dashboard.
- **market.png:** Three thick vertical financial candlesticks of different heights, short substantial wicks. Their three bodies form one compact rising market-chart composition. No enclosing chart panel or axes.
- **create.png:** One thick frosted silver coin disk, slightly angled, with a large simple raised plus sign centered on its face. The plus is bold and distinctly readable, no currency marks.
- **trade.png:** Two broad straight exchange arrows arranged horizontally one above the other. The upper arrow points RIGHT; the lower points LEFT. They are separated and unambiguously point opposite directions. Absolutely no coil, spring, spiral, curved loop or helix.
- **liquidity.png:** Two broad interlocking sculptural water droplets, one upright in front and another offset behind, unified in a compact liquidity-pair silhouette. Solid frosted smoke-silver, not clear bright blue water; simple quiet material.
- **leaderboard.png:** A compact championship trophy with a broad cup, two clearly visible side handles, a short sturdy stem and squared foot. Athletic award trophy, not a champagne goblet. No stars, numbers or writing.
- **registry.png:** One chunky rounded jigsaw puzzle piece with distinct connector tab and socket, representing modular software hooks. Near-front view, geometric and sophisticated, no shield or stack.
- **agents.png:** A single refined geometric robot head, rounded rectangular head, two simple recessed rectangular eye apertures, tiny central antenna, small side ear blocks. Professional automation assistant symbol, no smile or cartoon expression, no torso. Absolutely no coil, spring, spiral or helix.
- **docs.png:** One compact open reference book with two thick frosted silver page halves and a clear central spine, pages slightly fanned at lower edge. No writing or letters. Simple readable book silhouette.
