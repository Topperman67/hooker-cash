# Robinhood-inspired navigation

Nine original SVG symbols drawn for Hookbrew's charcoal and green interface. These are navigation artwork, not official Robinhood marks. Their 32-unit canvas, 1.8-unit round strokes, lime `#CCFF00`, and sage `#85C79A` accents keep them legible in the compact rail and expanded menu.

- Overview: house and doorway.
- Market: candlesticks.
- Create: brewing flask and plus.
- Trade: directional exchange arrows.
- Liquidity: droplet and pool wave.
- Leaderboard: podium and star.
- Hook registry: layered modules.
- Agents: automation network.
- Documentation: open book.

The prior platinum PNG artwork remains as historical assets. Active navigation uses these SVGs. Accessible names are supplied by the surrounding links; the images are decorative.
